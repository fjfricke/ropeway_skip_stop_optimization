# Interface Design Context

## Users

Transport researchers and optimization developers inspect scenarios, solver
artifacts, and cabin replays. They need dense evidence they can audit quickly,
including exact units, provenance, bounds, and safety diagnostics.

## Brand Personality

Technical, restrained, trustworthy. The interface should feel like an
engineering instrument rather than a marketing dashboard. Safety states use
conventional green, amber, and red signals, but never rely on color alone.

## Aesthetic Direction

Preserve the existing compact panel-based visual language, neutral surfaces,
strong typographic hierarchy, tabular numerics, and sparse functional color.
Avoid decorative gradients or motion except where they communicate state.

## Design Principles

1. Never present incomplete evidence as certified.
2. Put the verdict first and the quantities supporting it immediately below.
3. Keep raw model detail available without overwhelming the primary replay.
4. Use consistent physical units and stable labels throughout live and export views.
5. Make warning states legible through icon, text, and structure as well as color.
